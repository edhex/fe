Help us give students the things school doesn’t teach.

Thanks for being part of FutureEdge. We’re bringing together people who have performed, taught, led and coached at a high level so young Australians can borrow the lessons that usually take years to learn the hard way — the kind of guidance we wish we’d had while we were studying.

Why we’re asking

Your experience can shorten someone else’s learning curve.

We want the practical stuff: what worked, what didn’t, what you changed, what you wish you knew earlier — and the advice you’d genuinely give a student or parent today.

04

The simple agreement.

We want this to be understandable without a lawyer sitting beside you. Here’s what you’re actually agreeing to.

The short version

You keep ownership of what you give us. You let FutureEdge use your name, profile, photo and agreed contributions to make and promote FutureEdge study guides, events and related educational content. We can edit for length, clarity and presentation — including light image enhancement — but we won’t knowingly change what you mean or materially alter how you look.

What “your contribution” includes

Information you submit here and material you later choose to give FutureEdge during an interview or event — including your words, quotes, audio, video, image, profile details and photo.

Permission to use it

You give FutureEdge a non-exclusive, worldwide permission to record, edit, reproduce, publish, distribute and promote your agreed contribution in FutureEdge study guides, events, websites, social channels, presentations, media and related educational or promotional material.

You still own your material

This permission does not transfer ownership of your original contribution to FutureEdge. You can use your own material elsewhere.

Editing without changing the meaning

We may shorten, format, caption or lightly edit material for clarity, design or context. For images, that can include cropping, resizing, colour or lighting adjustment and minor retouching. We won’t knowingly present your views in a materially misleading way or materially change your appearance.

You can contact us

You can email us at any time to correct profile information, update your preferences, or ask us to stop future use or remove material where reasonably practicable. We’ll act reasonably and promptly, although we may not be able to recall material already printed, distributed or independently reposted by others.

No obligation either way

Submitting this form doesn’t guarantee FutureEdge will use your contribution, and it doesn’t require you to accept a future interview or event invitation.

If a contribution is paid

We’ll sort that separately and clearly. If a particular interview, study-guide contribution or event appearance involves a fee, we’ll agree the scope, amount and timing with you in writing before it happens. This form is simply about your profile and permission to use agreed FutureEdge material.

By submitting, you’re creating a record of the permissions above. If you want a copy, have a question, or want something changed later, email hello@myfutureedge.com. Any separately paid contribution will be confirmed with you in writing before that activity.
